Add `@effect/ai-amazon-bedrock`, an Amazon Bedrock provider for `effect/ai` that calls the Converse and ConverseStream APIs and signs requests with SigV4. It supports `generateText`, `streamText`, and `generateObject`, tool calling, image and document parts, document citations, prompt and tool-definition caching, and reasoning.
